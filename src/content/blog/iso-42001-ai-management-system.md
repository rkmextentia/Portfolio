### Why ISO 27001 Alone Is Not Enough for AI

Most mature organizations have ISO 27001 certifications. However, ISO 27001 was designed for information security (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability). It does not address:
- Fairness and algorithmic discrimination
- Model explainability and transparency
- Training data lineage and intellectual property risks

This is where **ISO/IEC 42001:2023** comes in as the world's first certifiable standard for **Artificial Intelligence Management Systems (AIMS)**.

---

### The Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) Cycle in ISO 42001

1. **Plan:** Establish organizational objectives for AI systems and identify ethical and regulatory risks.
2. **Do:** Implement AI impact assessments, data provenance tracking, and role-based training.
3. **Check:** Monitor AI system behavior against defined performance metrics and safety thresholds.
4. **Act:** Take corrective actions to retrain models, update prompt guardrails, or retire compromised systems.
