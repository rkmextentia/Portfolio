Core Advisory & Technical Capabilities

Pillar 1: AI Governance

AIGP & Global AI Regulation

Full lifecycle guidance to align machine learning architectures with binding international mandates:

  • EU AI Act: High-Risk classification, Article 50 watermarking, Annex III conformity.
  • FRIA: Fundamental Rights Impact Assessments for deployed models.
  • NIST AI RMF 1.0: Blueprint implementation across Govern, Map, Measure, Manage.
  • ISO/IEC 42001: Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS) setup.
Pillar 2: Enterprise GRC

Audit Assurance & Risk Programs

Transforming compliance from a checklist burden into a defensible competitive asset:

  • ISO 27001:2022 & SOC 2: Readiness audits, remediation, evidence pipelines.
  • Third-Party AI Risk (TPRM): Vendor evaluation questionnaires & model vetting.
  • Risk Registers: Quantitative risk scoring, KRI tracking, board reporting.
  • Internal AI Guardrails: Shadow AI policies and acceptable use guidelines.
Pillar 3: Corporate Training

Executive & Engineering Masterclasses

Pragmatic, scenario-based masterclasses designed to upskill cross-functional enterprise teams:

  • Executive & Boardroom: Strategic briefings on AI liabilities and legal roadmaps.
  • Engineering & Data Science: Red-teaming, prompt injection defense, bias testing.
  • Product Management: Embedding compliance into agile AI sprints and features.
  • Custom Masterclasses: Bespoke corporate upskilling sessions.
Pillar 4: SOC Governance

Security Operations Integration

Defensible incident management and security governance:

  • Alert Governance: AI incident response protocols and forensic audit readiness.
  • Model Monitoring: Continuous drift, latency, and jailbreak detection alerts.
  • Regulatory Reporting: Meeting mandatory 72-hour AI incident notification rules.
